## About

I am a Data Engineer with two years of professional experience across big data platforms, real-time processing, and scalable system design, currently pursuing my MS in Data Science at The George Washington University. Previously I worked as a Junior Data Analyst, building the habit of connecting data work to concrete business outcomes.

My day-to-day interests are the full data lifecycle: ingestion under validated contracts, medallion modeling with dbt, observable orchestration, and BI on top of clean, tested marts. On the side I work on applied machine learning for computer vision and NLP.

---

## Currently

- **Learning deeply:** modern lakehouse architectures, declarative orchestration (Dagster, Prefect), and data contracts at scale.
- **Building:** `retail-analytics-pipeline`, an end-to-end data engineering portfolio project covering ingestion, medallion modeling, dashboarding, and CI.
- **Reading:** *Fundamentals of Data Engineering* (Reis and Housley) and *The Data Warehouse Toolkit* (Kimball).
- **Open to:** full-time Data Engineer, Analytics Engineer, and ML-adjacent roles starting 2026.
